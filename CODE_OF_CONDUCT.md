# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is acting in good faith. Criticize ideas, not people.

## What is expected

- Keep discussion focused on the work.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change and still be right to.
- Give people room to be new at something.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or deliberate bad faith.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere someone is representing this project in public.

## Enforcement

Report problems to **hello@ildana.ai**. Reports are handled privately, and the reporter's identity is not shared.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this document, and may block accounts for repeated or serious violations. Enforcement decisions are the maintainers' to make.

This is adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), shortened for a project this size.
